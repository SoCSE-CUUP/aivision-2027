Conference Events

The International Conference on Artificial Intelligence and Visual Intelligence (AIVISION 2027) will feature a variety of events including paper presentations, workshops, special sessions.

Call for Conference Papers

We invite original research contributions in the field of secure and trustworthy AI systems. All submissions will be peer-reviewed and selected based on their originality, technical quality, and relevance to the conference theme.

Submission Deadline: Jan 30, 2027

Learn More

Call for Workshops

Proposals are invited for workshops to be organized in conjunction with AIVISION 2027. Each workshop will provide a dedicated platform to explore specialized topics and emerging developments in Artificial Intelligence, Computer Vision and Visual Intelligence. Workshops will encourage knowledge sharing, technical discussions and research collaboration among academicians, researchers, industry professionals and technology innovators.

Proposal Submission Deadline: Feb 20, 2027
