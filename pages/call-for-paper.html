Call for Conference Papers

The International Conference on Artificial Intelligence and Visual Intelligence (AIVISION 2027) is an international forum where researchers, academicians, technology innovators, industry experts, entrepreneurs, policy makers and students are invited to present and share their views on the latest developments in Artificial Intelligence and Visual Intelligence. The objective of the conference is to unite and share research trends, innovative methodologies, advanced computational models and applications of AI-powered visual technologies in scientific, engineering and industrial, healthcare, agricultural, environmental, financial, educational and societal research.

AIVISION 2027 is aware that Artificial Intelligence plays a more and more crucial role in the acquisition, processing, interpretation and use of visual information for intelligent decision making. Visual intelligence is becoming a major research area, thanks to the exponential growth of computer vision, deep learning, vision transformers, generative AI and multimodal learning, and is seeing applications in a wide range of fields. The conference is designed to foster an interdisciplinary research platform providing an opportunity for exchange of ideas, presentation of innovative research, build connections with emerging technologies and solve complex real-life problems using intelligent visual computing.

The conference will provide an overview of the latest advancements in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, Deep Learning Architectures, Computer Vision, Image Processing, Pattern Recognition, Image Classification, Object Detection, and Image Segmentation, as well as Video Analytics, Vision Transformers, Generative AI, Large Vision-Language Models, Multimodal Artificial Intelligence, Visual Representation Learning, Explainable AI, and Intelligent Visual Recognition. It will also stimulate new research in new applications like autonomous visual systems, visual reasoning, synthetic image generation, 3D visions, medical image analysis, remote sensing and intelligent visual decision support systems.

Scope and Topics of Interest

AIVISION 2027 solicits original research contributions, comprehensive review articles, and position papers addressing recent advancements, emerging challenges, and innovative applications in Artificial Intelligence, Computer Vision, and Visual Intelligence. Relevant topics include, but are not limited to:

Track 1: Foundations of Artificial Intelligence and Intelligent Computing

  • Foundations and evolution of Artificial Intelligence: Concepts, methods and applications
  • Mathematical and computational foundations of intelligent systems
  • Supervised, unsupervised and reinforcement learning approaches
  • Deep learning fundamentals and advanced neural architectures
  • Representation learning and feature engineering for intelligent systems
  • Transfer learning and domain adaptation for visual applications
  • Self-supervised and semi-supervised learning for visual data
  • Ensemble learning and hybrid Artificial Intelligence models
  • Optimization techniques for intelligent computing systems
  • Emerging trends and future directions in Artificial Intelligence

Track 2: Deep Learning Architectures and Vision Transformers

  • Convolutional Neural Networks for advanced visual recognition
  • Vision Transformers: Architectures, optimization and applications
  • Hybrid CNN–Transformer architectures for visual intelligence
  • Attention mechanisms and feature fusion in deep neural networks
  • Lightweight and efficient deep learning models for edge vision
  • Self-attention and cross-attention mechanisms for visual representation
  • Few-shot and zero-shot learning for visual recognition
  • Foundation models for image and video understanding
  • Neural architecture search for computer vision applications
  • Robust and scalable deep learning architectures for visual computing

Track 3: Computer Vision, Image Processing and Pattern Recognition

  • Advanced image processing techniques and computational methods
  • Image enhancement, restoration and super-resolution
  • Image classification and fine-grained visual recognition
  • Object detection, localization and instance segmentation
  • Semantic segmentation and scene parsing
  • Feature extraction and visual representation learning
  • Texture analysis, shape recognition and visual descriptors
  • Image registration, reconstruction and 3D computer vision
  • Medical and industrial image processing techniques
  • Advanced pattern recognition using machine learning and deep learning

Track 4: Visual Recognition, Video Analytics and Scene Understanding

  • Intelligent video analytics and real-time visual monitoring
  • Human activity recognition and behaviour analysis
  • Object tracking and multi-object tracking systems
  • Facial expression, gesture and emotion recognition
  • Scene understanding and visual relationship detection
  • Action recognition and event detection in video streams
  • Anomaly detection in images and video surveillance
  • Autonomous visual navigation and scene perception
  • Multi-camera vision and spatio-temporal analysis
  • Intelligent surveillance and smart visual monitoring systems

Track 5: Generative AI, Multimodal Intelligence and Vision-Language Models

  • Generative Artificial Intelligence for image and video synthesis
  • Generative Adversarial Networks and diffusion-based models
  • Image-to-image translation and visual content generation
  • Vision-Language Models for image and video understanding
  • Multimodal learning across text, images, video and sensor data
  • Visual question answering and image captioning
  • Cross-modal representation learning and information retrieval
  • Multimodal Large Language Models for visual reasoning
  • Synthetic data generation and data augmentation for computer vision
  • Generative AI for creative, scientific and industrial applications

Track 6: Explainable, Trustworthy and Responsible Visual Intelligence

  • Explainable Artificial Intelligence for computer vision systems
  • Interpretable deep learning models for visual recognition
  • Explainable object detection and image classification
  • Model transparency and visual explanation techniques
  • Uncertainty quantification in deep learning-based vision systems
  • Adversarial robustness and security of computer vision models
  • Bias detection and fairness in visual recognition systems
  • Privacy-preserving computer vision and federated learning
  • Trustworthy AI for safety-critical visual applications
  • Ethical, responsible and human-centric visual intelligence

Track 7: AI Applications in Healthcare, Biomedical and Agricultural Sciences

  • Deep learning for medical image analysis and diagnosis
  • AI-based cancer detection and biomedical image classification
  • Explainable AI for clinical decision support
  • Computer vision for histopathology and cytological image analysis
  • Biomedical signal and medical visual data analytics
  • AI-based crop disease and agricultural pest detection
  • Computer vision for weed detection and precision agriculture
  • Intelligent crop monitoring and agricultural yield assessment
  • AI-driven healthcare monitoring and disease prediction
  • Visual intelligence for biomedical and agricultural decision support

Track 8: Industrial Vision, Environmental Monitoring and Autonomous Systems

  • Automated visual inspection and industrial quality assessment
  • Deep learning for defect detection and surface inspection
  • Intelligent manufacturing and vision-based quality control
  • AI-based structural and infrastructure monitoring
  • Remote sensing and satellite image analysis
  • Computer vision for land use and land cover classification
  • AI-based environmental and climate monitoring
  • Visual intelligence for disaster detection and risk assessment
  • Autonomous vehicles and intelligent transportation systems
  • Robotics, visual navigation and human–robot interaction

Track 9: Visual Intelligence for Smart Cities and Emerging Applications

  • AI-based traffic monitoring and intelligent transportation
  • Smart infrastructure monitoring using computer vision
  • Visual analytics for urban planning and smart cities
  • AI-driven surveillance and public safety systems
  • Visual intelligence for retail and customer behaviour analytics
  • Computer vision applications in education and e-learning
  • Visual recognition for financial and business intelligence
  • AI-based document image analysis and recognition
  • Edge AI and IoT-enabled visual intelligence
  • Emerging applications of intelligent visual computing

Track 10: Data Analytics, Optimization and Decision Support for Visual AI

  • Big data analytics for computer vision applications
  • Feature selection and dimensionality reduction for visual data
  • Explainable predictive analytics for intelligent decision-making
  • Time-series and spatio-temporal visual data analysis
  • Anomaly detection and risk assessment using AI
  • Hybrid machine learning frameworks for visual intelligence
  • Optimization of deep learning models for real-time applications
  • Distributed and cloud-based visual computing
  • Edge computing and resource-efficient AI inference
  • Intelligent decision support systems using multimodal visual data

Submission Guidelines

Papers must be submitted through the Microsoft CMT system. Please follow these guidelines:

  • Submissions must be original and not published or under review elsewhere
  • Papers should be formatted according to Springer SIST style
  • Full papers: 5-6 pages
  • All submissions will undergo rigorous peer review by at least three experts
