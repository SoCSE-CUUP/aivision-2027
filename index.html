Conference Theme

"Advancing Artificial Intelligence and Visual Intelligence for a Smarter, More Connected Future"

At AIVISION 2027, researchers, academicians, industry professionals, technology innovators and visionaries from across the world come together to explore the transformative potential of Artificial Intelligence and Visual Intelligence. As intelligent technologies continue to reshape the way we perceive, interpret and interact with the world, this conference provides an international platform for sharing innovative ideas, groundbreaking research and emerging technological developments. Bringing together diverse perspectives and expertise, AIVISION 2027 aims to bridge the gap between theoretical advancements and practical applications, fostering innovation through intelligent visual computing.

Visual intelligence is redefining the boundaries of modern technology. From machines that recognize and understand complex visual environments to intelligent systems capable of generating images, interpreting videos and supporting critical decisions, AI-powered vision technologies are opening new possibilities across disciplines. Advances in computer vision, deep learning, vision transformers, generative AI and multimodal intelligence are creating opportunities in healthcare, agriculture, industrial automation, smart cities, environmental monitoring and autonomous systems. At the same time, challenges involving accuracy, interpretability, reliability, scalability and responsible deployment demand continued research and collaboration.

AIVISION 2027 places these emerging opportunities and challenges at the centre of its discussions. The conference encourages researchers to explore innovative architectures, intelligent algorithms, explainable models and real-world visual applications that can address complex societal and industrial problems. Through keynote lectures, research presentations and expert interactions, the event seeks to inspire collaboration, advance scientific knowledge and shape the future of trustworthy, efficient and human-centric Visual Intelligence.

About the Conference

The International Conference on Artificial Intelligence and Visual Intelligence (AIVISION 2027) is an international forum where researchers, academicians, technology innovators, industry experts, entrepreneurs, policy makers and students are invited to present and share their views on the latest developments in Artificial Intelligence and Visual Intelligence. The objective of the conference is to unite and share research trends, innovative methodologies, advanced computational models and applications of AI-powered visual technologies in scientific, engineering and industrial, healthcare, agricultural, environmental, financial, educational and societal research.

The conference aims to maintain a balance between theory, methodology, experimentation and technology. It will provide a platform for researchers to share new algorithms, computational models, hybrid architectures, experimental studies, case studies, prototypes and application-oriented solutions. The programme will feature keynote speeches, technical paper presentations, expert panel discussions, demonstrations, poster presentations and networking opportunities to ensure meaningful academic and professional engagement.

AIVISION 2027 will provide a forum for meaningful scientific exchange, collaborative research, knowledge sharing and technological advancement, highlighting the diverse dimensions of AI, vision and innovation. It aims to contribute to the development of reliable, explainable, efficient and application-oriented Artificial Intelligence and Visual Intelligence solutions.

About Centre of Excellence

The Centre of Excellence for Cybersecurity Privacy and Data Protection Interdisciplinary Hub at Chandigarh University was founded to push forward advanced studies, inventive thinking, and growth in practical abilities, among key domains tied to safeguarding digital systems. Created following a clear intent to build stronger national skills while encouraging partnerships between businesses and universities

The Centre works on advanced topics like Network Security, App Security, Cloud Protection, Device Safeguards, Monitoring Risks, Intelligence Digital Forensics, Post Quantum Cryptography, Zero Trust, and Buildings meet device safety. Where structures stand, machines connect securely.

Established through collaboration among international experts focused on digital security Leaders Palo Alto Networks, CheckPoint, Fortinet, CyberPeace Foundation - the Centre brings together academia, industry, and civil society to face new issues in safeguarding digital information, ensuring personal data stays secure, yet respecting individual privacy remains complex. Working with cutting-edge tools shapes how challenges get tackled outside textbooks. Solving actual issues grounds learning in experience rather than theory alone. Doing things physically builds understanding beyond what reading achieves. Through training, the Centre empowers students, researchers, and professionals to meet new digital dangers, which require homegrown strategies. Solutions emerge when local expertise tackles shifting risks. Facing modern attacks means building tools from within. Home-built responses adapt faster to changing threats. Creating internal methods strengthens defense over time.

With its unique blend of expertise and resources, the Centre is committed to creating impactful solutions that safeguard digital ecosystems, strengthen cyber resilience, and contribute to a secure and trustworthy cyberspace.
